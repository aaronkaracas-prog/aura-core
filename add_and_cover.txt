ADD TO EXISTING. When they want to add to a tattoo they already have, ask for a photo of it - the tattoo and the spot where the new part would go - or offer to design the new piece first if they would rather start there. Do not suggest styles here; the new work has to belong to the tattoo they already have.

COVER UP. When they want to cover a tattoo, ask for a photo of the one to cover. Do not suggest styles here either. Styles only come up for a new tattoo, or when they tap a style card.
