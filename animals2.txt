GLOBAL ANIMALS GENERATION PROMPT

Create exceptional contemporary tattoo inspiration centered on animals.

ANIMALS = WHAT I LOVE.

This category is about fascination with animals themselves.

Their movement.

Anatomy.

Behavior.

Texture.

Environment.

Power.

Beauty.

Strangeness.

Violence.

Scale.

Survival.

DO NOT GENERATE AN ANIMAL PORTRAIT WHEN AN ANIMAL EXPERIENCE WOULD BE MORE INTERESTING.

Do not default to a centered animal head looking directly at the viewer.

Show animals behaving like animals.

Running.

Hunting.

Swimming.

Diving.

Flying.

Landing.

Climbing.

Stalking.

Feeding.

Fighting.

Resting.

Migrating.

Shedding.

Emerging.

Disappearing.

Turning.

Striking.

Escaping.

Moving through environments.

DO NOT MAKE THE ANIMAL BEAUTIFUL BY DEFAULT.

MAKE IT ALIVE.

Beauty is welcome.

But beauty is not the requirement.

Animals can be:

Elegant.

Violent.

Quiet.

Predatory.

Ugly.

Strange.

Ancient.

Fragile.

Massive.

Graceful.

Armored.

Wet.

Dirty.

Wounded.

Hidden.

Colorful.

Dark.

Terrifying.

Peaceful.

Do not romanticize every animal.

Do not turn every predator into a noble portrait.

Do not turn every bird into wings spread symmetrically.

Do not turn every snake into a decorative coil.

Do not turn every ocean animal into a peaceful blue scene.

Do not turn every wildlife tattoo into flowers surrounding an animal head.

THE STYLE IS NOT FIXED.

Use the full world of tattooing.

Realism.

Black and Grey Realism.

Colour Realism.

Fine Line.

Sketch.

Dotwork.

Blackwork.

Watercolour.

Illustrative.

Geometric.

Japanese.

American Traditional.

Neo-Traditional.

New School.

Negative Space.

Surrealism.

And intelligent hybrids.

Do not rotate styles mechanically.

Ask what the animal gives the artist.

Fur can become brushwork.

Feathers can become Blackwork.

Scales can become geometry.

Spots can become Dotwork.

Stripes can become abstraction.

Tentacles can become body flow.

Wings can become large negative-space compositions.

Shells and armor can become texture studies.

Translucent animals can become Colour Realism and Watercolour.

White animals can be created through untouched skin.

Black animals can emerge from darkness.

THE ANIMAL'S ANATOMY SHOULD HELP DESIGN THE TATTOO.

A snake can wrap.

An octopus can travel around anatomy.

A manta ray can own a back.

A bird can use the shoulder blades.

A giraffe can use vertical anatomy.

A crocodile can travel down a leg.

A whale can establish enormous scale.

A stag can expand through antlers.

A jellyfish can descend through long placements.

A big cat can move across the thigh.

A raven can use shoulder and chest.

Do not put every animal inside a rectangular scene.

Allow environment and animal to interlock.

Use:

Snow.

Water.

Fog.

Dust.

Rain.

Grass.

Forest.

Rock.

Desert.

Ocean.

Swamp.

Night.

Fire.

Wind.

Light.

Shadow.

Environment should reveal something about the animal.

It should not simply become decorative background.

REGIONAL WILDLIFE MATTERS.

People may love animals because those animals belong to where they are from.

Do not assume every animal requires universal symbolism.

A coyote can matter because someone grew up hearing coyotes.

A pelican can matter because someone lives on the coast.

An alligator can matter because it belongs to someone's landscape.

A whale can matter because someone sees them migrate.

The animal does not need a dictionary meaning to deserve a tattoo.

DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY ASSIGN CANNED SYMBOLISM.

Lion does not automatically mean courage.

Wolf does not automatically mean loyalty.

Owl does not automatically mean wisdom.

Snake does not automatically mean rebirth.

If symbolism is important, let the customer define it or explain genuine cultural associations accurately.

Nature can become dark without becoming Horror.

Explore:

Predation.

Death.

Decay.

Shedding.

Skeletons.

Migration.

Survival.

Carcasses.

Scavengers.

Deep water.

Darkness.

Injury.

Transformation.

Weather.

Extinction.

Life cycles.

Keep the animal world central.

Do not add skulls and demons simply to make an animal tattoo darker.

NATURE ALREADY CONTAINS DARKNESS.

Surrealism is welcome when the animal remains the hero.

A whale can contain a storm.

A stag's antlers can become forest.

A leopard's spots can become stars.

A zebra's stripes can leave its body.

A bird's wings can contain night.

A rhinoceros can become landscape.

A manta ray can pass over an impossible city.

A snake can become smoke.

But do not bury the animal beneath an unrelated concept.

The viewer should still think:

WHAT AN INCREDIBLE ANIMAL TATTOO.

Design for actual human anatomy.

Use the body.

Wrap when the animal gives you wrapping anatomy.

Stretch when the animal gives you length.

Expand when the animal gives you wings.

Use joints when movement benefits from them.

Use broad surfaces for scale.

Use small placements for delicate animals.

Use large placements only when the concept earns them.

Do not make everything enormous.

Do not make everything tiny.

Do not make everything realistic.

Do not make everything Blackwork.

Do not make everything colorful.

Do not make everything dark.

Do not make everything pretty.

Do not make everything safe.

THE CATEGORY TELLS US THE DOORWAY.

IT DOES NOT TELL US HOW FAR WE ARE ALLOWED TO GO ONCE WE ARE INSIDE.

THE ANIMAL IS THE HERO.

THE ART CAN GO ANYWHERE.


CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION


WHAT IS IT?

Animals is tattoo inspiration built around animals you find fascinating.

Wildlife.

Predators.

Birds.

Ocean life.

Reptiles.

Amphibians.

Strange creatures.

Prehistoric animals.

Regional wildlife.

And almost anything else in the animal world.

You do not need a symbolic reason.

You can simply think the animal is incredible.


WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE?

Anything.

Realistic.

Black and Grey.

Colorful.

Fine Line.

Sketch.

Dotwork.

Blackwork.

Watercolour.

Illustrative.

Geometric.

Traditional.

Neo-Traditional.

New School.

Surreal.

Minimal.

Massive.

Dark.

Beautiful.

Strange.

The animal can be a portrait.

But it can also run, hunt, swim, fly, hide, climb, strike, dive or disappear into its environment.


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT?

The closest category is Pets.

But the reason for entering each category is different.

PETS = WHO I LOVE.

ANIMALS = WHAT I LOVE.

If you want your Labrador because he has been beside you for twelve years, explore Pets.

If you want a wolf because wolves fascinate you, explore Animals.

If you want your horse, explore Pets.

If you want a wild herd of horses running through dust, explore Animals.

Pets starts with a relationship to one particular animal.

Animals starts with fascination with the animal itself.


WHAT DOES IT WORK WELL FOR?

Wildlife.

Big cats.

Predators.

Birds.

Ocean animals.

Reptiles.

Amphibians.
