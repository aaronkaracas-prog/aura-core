TATTOO EXTRACTION — SKIN TO FLAT ARTWORK

TASK:
Extract the tattoo artwork visible on the person's skin and reconstruct it as clean, flat, tattoo-artist-ready artwork.

The tattoo in the supplied image is the source of truth.

GOAL:
Return the tattoo design itself, removed from the human body and presented flat on a clean plain white background.

PRESERVE:
- The complete tattoo design
- Original composition
- Original proportions
- Original orientation
- Original linework
- Original shapes
- Original imagery
- Original lettering and spelling
- Original symbols
- Original ornamentation
- Original colors
- Original shading
- Original relative spacing between elements
- Intentional asymmetry
- Fine details that are visibly part of the tattoo

REMOVE:
- Skin
- Body
- Body contours
- Anatomical curvature
- Perspective caused by the body
- Clothing
- Hair
- Background
- Lighting and shadows caused by the body/environment
- Skin texture
- Pores
- Redness
- Glare
- Photographic artifacts

FLATTEN:
Interpret the tattoo as artwork wrapped around a three-dimensional human surface.

Correct the perspective and anatomical curvature so the resulting design appears as it would on a flat drawing surface.

Where parts of the tattoo curve around the body, reconstruct their flat geometry conservatively using the visible tattoo as evidence.

Do not retain the body's distortion in the flattened artwork.

CRITICAL:
This is an extraction and reconstruction task, NOT a tattoo redesign.

Do not:
- improve the design
- beautify the design
- add decorative elements
- remove tattoo elements
- make it more symmetrical
- change lettering
- change colors
- change the artistic style
- invent details unnecessarily
- substitute similar imagery
- reinterpret the tattoo

If an area is partially obscured or distorted by anatomy, reconstruct only what is reasonably implied by the visible artwork and surrounding design.

Maintain the identity of the original tattoo as closely as possible.

OUTPUT:
- Tattoo artwork only
- No person
- No skin
- No body
- No mockup
- No environmental background
- Plain white background
- Centered
- Entire design visible
- Flat, front-facing presentation
- High detail
- Clean edges
- Maximum useful resolution
- Suitable as a tattoo artist reference

The final image should look like the original tattoo artwork before it was applied to the person's body.
